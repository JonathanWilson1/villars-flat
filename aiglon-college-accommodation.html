Aiglon College accommodation for visiting families

A family-sized apartment in Villars, on the same road as the Aiglon campus, for parents' weekends, exeats, arrivals and graduation.

Hotels in Villars fill quickly on school weekends, and one room gets small fast with a teenager and their friends. Le Bristol A46 is a one-bedroom apartment for up to 4, with two bathrooms, two balconies, a kitchenette and a heated indoor pool in the building. It's a quiet base where the family can spread out, with Interhome's local Villars office just along the road if you need anything.

Where it is, compared with Aiglon

Aiglon College's campus is at Avenue Centrale 61 in Chesières, the neighbouring village just down the hill from Villars. Le Bristol is further up the same road, at Avenue Centrale 164 in Villars. That's roughly 1.5 km: a few minutes by car, or the local bus, which stops right beside the school (stop: Chesières – Aiglon College).

So you're close enough to drop in for a match, a concert or a quick lunch, while staying in the middle of Villars, near its shops, restaurants and the station.

Getting to Villars

  • From Geneva Airport by train: direct trains run along Lake Geneva to Bex, then the little mountain railway climbs up to Villars. Allow about 2 hours.
  • By car: about 1 hour 30 minutes from Geneva, leaving the motorway at Aigle or Bex. In winter, check whether you need snow chains.
  • From Zurich: allow about 3 hours by train.

Questions families ask

How far is the apartment from Aiglon College?

Roughly 1.5 km along Avenue Centrale, the main road through Chesières and Villars. It's a few minutes by car, or take the local bus, which stops beside the school.

How many people does it sleep?

Up to 4: two single beds in the bedroom and a double sofa bed in the living room. There are two bathrooms.

Can we arrive by train?

Yes. The apartment is about 500 m from Villars station, and from Geneva Airport the train journey takes about 2 hours.

When should we book for exeats and graduation?

As soon as the school publishes its calendar. Dates around the start and end of term, exeats and graduation are the first to go in Villars, especially in February during the ski season.

Who handles the booking?

Interhome, the holiday-rental company, handles booking and payment. Their local office in Villars, on Avenue Centrale, looks after keys, cleaning and any help you need during your stay.
