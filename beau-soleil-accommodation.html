Where it is, compared with Beau Soleil

Beau Soleil (Collège Alpin International Beau Soleil) is on Route du Village in the centre of Villars-sur-Ollon. Le Bristol is at Avenue Centrale 164, about 500 m from the village centre, so the school is roughly a 10-minute walk away.

You can leave the car parked and walk to the school, to the village shops and restaurants, and to Villars station.

Getting to Villars

  • From Geneva Airport by train: direct trains run along Lake Geneva to Bex, then the little mountain railway climbs up to Villars. Allow about 2 hours.
  • By car: about 1 hour 30 minutes from Geneva, leaving the motorway at Aigle or Bex. In winter, check whether you need snow chains.
  • From Zurich: allow about 3 hours by train.

Questions families ask

How far is the apartment from Beau Soleil?

Beau Soleil is on Route du Village in the centre of Villars. The apartment is about 500 m from the village centre, so the walk takes roughly 10 minutes.

How many people does it sleep?

Up to 4: two single beds in the bedroom and a double sofa bed in the living room. There are two bathrooms.

Do we need a car?

No. The school, the shops and Villars station are all within walking distance, and in winter the free ski bus stops 20 m from the building.

When should we book?

As soon as the school calendar is out. The start and end of term, half-terms and graduation fill Villars quickly, especially in the February ski season.

Who handles the booking?

Interhome, the holiday-rental company, handles booking, payment, key collection and cleaning.
