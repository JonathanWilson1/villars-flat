The apartment

Morning coffee with a view across the Alps

On the fourth floor of Le Bristol, the apartment looks out over the valley to the peaks beyond. Two balconies catch the sun from morning to evening. Inside, warm pine walls and new floors make it a cosy base in any season.

The bus stop is at the door, the shops and restaurants are 9 minutes' walk away, and at the end of the day there's a heated indoor pool downstairs.

Ski

Over 100 km of pistes

Villars–Gryon–Les Diablerets links wide blue and red runs with views to Mont Blanc. The free ski bus stops 20 m from the building.

Getting up

The train to Bretaye

The little rack railway climbs from Villars station, 500 m from the flat, straight up to Col de Bretaye at the heart of the ski area. Free with a valid ski pass.

Getting up

Roc d'Orsay gondola

Or take the gondola from the village up to Roc d'Orsay. Lockers, ski hire and ski schools for children from age 3 are all close by.

Not skiing?

Winter walks & snowshoes

Groomed winter footpaths and snowshoe trails start from the village and from Bretaye. Sledging and an ice rink too.

Après-ski

Pool downstairs

Come back, drop the skis in the ski room and swim in the heated indoor pool in the building. Then fondue on the balcony.

Day trip

Glacier 3000

About 40 minutes' drive via Les Diablerets: a cable car to the glacier, the Peak Walk suspension bridge and skiing at 3,000 m.

Walk

The Bretaye lakes

Ride the little train from Villars station up to Col de Bretaye, then walk round Lac de Bretaye and Lac Noir, or 30–40 minutes on to Lac des Chavonnes and its lakeside terrace restaurant.

Walk

Trails for every level

Family strolls in the larch woods, the Edelweiss Trail, or up Le Chamossaire for the panorama. Bikes go on the Bretaye train, and can be hired in the village.

Play

Golf at altitude

Villars golf course, an 18-hole mountain course, is 7 km away. The village sports centre has tennis, a pool and an ice rink.

Relax

Pools & thermal baths

The heated pool is downstairs. For a treat, the Lavey thermal baths in the Rhône valley are about 25 minutes' drive, and the village sports centre has a spa.

Day trip

Bex salt mines

Ride a small train deep into a working salt mine, a hit with children. About 20 minutes' drive down to Bex; tours take about 2 hours, so book online.

Day trip

Lake Geneva & Chillon

Montreux and Château de Chillon on the lake are about 40 minutes away. Swim, take a paddle steamer, and be back for sunset on the balcony.
