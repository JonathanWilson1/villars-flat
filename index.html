The apartment

Morning coffee with a view across the Alps

On the fourth floor of Le Bristol, the apartment looks out over the valley to the peaks beyond. Two balconies catch the sun from morning to evening. Inside, warm pine walls and new floors make it a cosy base in any season.

The bus stop is at the door, the shops and restaurants are 9 minutes' walk away, and at the end of the day there's a heated indoor pool downstairs.

Inside

Warm, light, unmistakably alpine

Every season

Summer meadows, winter pistes

Summer

Hiking and mountain-bike trails from the village, an 18-hole golf course 7 km away, the Lavey thermal baths and Lake Geneva below. Then dinner on the balcony as the light fades.

Winter

The free ski bus stops 20 m from the door, and there's a ski room downstairs. Villars–Gryon–Diablerets has about 100 km of runs, ski schools from age 3 and an ice rink in the village.
