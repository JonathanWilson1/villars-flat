Inside the apartment

Summer

Hiking and mountain-bike trails from the village, an 18-hole golf course 7 km away, the Lavey thermal baths 19 km away and Lake Geneva below. Then dinner on the balcony as the sun goes down over the Dents du Midi.

Winter

The free ski bus stops 20 m from the door and there's a ski room in the building. The Villars–Gryon–Diablerets area has about 100 km of runs, ski schools from age 3, an ice rink and toboggan runs.
